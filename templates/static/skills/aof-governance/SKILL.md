# AOF Governance

Use this skill for changes touching AI automation, Authority, Policy, Risk, Approval, State, consequential effects, Evidence, or Verification.

Treat AI output as an UntrustedProposal until applicable governance evaluation occurs. Capability does not imply Authority. Approval does not create AuthorityGrant. Deferred or unresolved mandatory controls do not mean Pass.
