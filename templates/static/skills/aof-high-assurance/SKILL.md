# AOF High Assurance

Use when the High-Assurance overlay is selected or Critical/high-impact effects demand stronger controls.

Require strong separation of duties, independent Verification, explicit approval for Critical actions, stronger Evidence, strict Authority scope, Effect Boundary revalidation, protected Trace, bounded Recovery, and residual Risk handling. High-Assurance strengthens and never replaces the applicable base/domain profile.
